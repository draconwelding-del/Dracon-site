Building the shop in Burin

A 40 x 40 ft welding and fabrication shop at 5A Ropewalk Lane. The building will be complete in Q1 2027, we will move in during Q2 2027, and Dracon services will begin in Q3 2027.

Where it stands

The slab was poured in June 2026. Since then the walls have been framed and stood, the trusses are on, and the steel siding, windows and man door are going in. Photos of the build will be added here.

The plans

The building

40 x 40 ft with 14 ft walls, about 1,600 sq ft. Two 12 x 12 ft overhead bay doors will face the driveway, with a 7 x 9 ft overhead door and a man door at the back, plus a front man door. A small 11 x 9 ft compressor room is planned on the back, reached from outside through a 5x6 overhead door.

Heating

A cold-climate ducted heat pump will be the main heat, rated to run down to -25 C. Air will be thrown high across the shop toward the bay doors, so the weld bays stay calm for shielding gas. A 5 kW electric unit heater will back it up after the bay doors open and in deep cold. The shop will be held at 15 to 18 C, and a small heater will keep the compressor room above freezing.

Electrical

The shop will have a 400 A single-phase main panel. Each machine will get its own circuit: the plasma table, fiber laser, compressor and dryer, and three 50 A welding outlets across two weld bays. All wiring will run inside the walls with no ceiling drops, and LED high-bay lights will light the floor. Final sizes will come from the electrician's load calculation.

Compressed air

A 10 HP variable-speed screw compressor will sit in its own room with a refrigerated dryer and a 120 gal tank, keeping the noise and heat out of the shop. Clean, dry air will be piped to the fiber laser and the tools.

Ventilation

Two low intake louvers on one wall will feed two high exhaust fans on the other, moving about 2,300 CFM, around 6 air changes an hour. Air will cross both weld bays side to side, away from the welder. A fume extractor with a 10 ft arm will capture fume right at the weld. The general fans will clear the shop air; they will not replace capture at the source.

Plans are drafts and will be confirmed on site and by the trades doing the work.
