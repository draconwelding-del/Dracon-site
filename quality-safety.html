Quality and safety

Safe work and documented quality, built in from the start.

Safety

Dracon will be registered with WorkplaceNL. NLCSA SiteSafe enrolment is planned, and a full Certificate of Recognition (COR) is targeted for about 2028.

Quality

CWB W47.1 and ISO 9001 are the path forward; not yet held. An ISO 9001 quality manual is in draft, and welding will follow documented procedures.

Client procedures

Pipe and pressure work will be done only under a client's own procedures. Other certifications will be pursued when a customer or contract requires them.

Insurance

$2M liability insurance will be in place before the Burin shop move-in.
