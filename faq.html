Frequently asked questions

Short answers about Dracon and the Burin shop. Services begin Q3 2027.

When will Dracon open?

The shop building will be complete in Q1 2027, we will move in during Q2 2027, and Dracon services will begin in Q3 2027.

Where is the shop?

5A Ropewalk Lane, Burin, NL A0E 1E0. You can follow its progress on the Shop build page.

Can you take work now?

No. Nothing is offered or available before Q3 2027. If you think you may have work for us, you are welcome to register your interest. It is non-binding.

What work will Dracon do?

Planned services include custom fabrication; structural and miscellaneous steel; aluminum and stainless fabrication; CNC fiber laser cutting, laser welding, engraving and marking, and CNC plasma cutting; repair and maintenance welding; and design work through K2 Designs Ltd. See Planned services for details.

Will you do pipe or pressure work?

Pipe and pressure work will be done only under a client's own procedures.

Are you certified?

Not yet. CWB W47.1 and ISO 9001 are the path forward; neither is held yet. Dracon will be registered with WorkplaceNL before opening. See Quality & Safety.

Will you travel for field work?

The shop will be based in Burin and will serve the Burin Peninsula. Field work further afield will be considered case by case.

Will you sell products?

Laser-cut signs, parts and fire pits are planned future products. Nothing is for sale now. See Planned products.

What is K2 Designs?

K2 Designs Ltd, a Dracon Group company (incorporation in progress), is where Dracon's design work will be done.
