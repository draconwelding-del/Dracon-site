Planned services

Services begin Q3 2027: fabrication, repair and cutting for contractors, industry and local customers.

Custom fabrication

Frames, skids, brackets, guards, chutes, wear plates and custom parts, to be made to print or reverse-engineered from an existing part. Assemblies will be shop-built for installation in the field.

Structural and miscellaneous steel

Platforms, stairs, handrails, frames and skids. Structural plate connections will be bolted until CWB W47.1 certification is in place.

Aluminum and stainless fabrication

Careful work in aluminum and stainless steel for parts, enclosures, racks and custom builds.

CNC laser cutting, laser welding, engraving and plasma cutting

From Q3 2027 the Burin shop will run an xTool MetalFab 1200W fiber laser welder and CNC cutter. On its 610 x 610 mm (24" x 24") cutting bed, with pass-through for longer sheet, it cuts carbon steel and galvanized sheet up to 10 mm (3/8") in one pass, stainless steel up to 5 mm, aluminum up to 4 mm and brass up to 3 mm. It also handles handheld laser welding of stainless and carbon steel up to 5 mm, handheld cutting, laser rust removal and post-weld cleaning.

For fine work, an xTool F2 Ultra 60W MOPA fiber laser will handle metal engraving and full-colour marking on stainless steel and titanium, and cut thin stainless steel and brass up to 2 mm and aluminum up to 1 mm. Its bed is 220 x 220 mm, or 220 x 500 mm with the conveyor for engraving batches, and an xTool RA3 rotary will be added for tumblers and other round items.

A Creality Falcon2 Pro 40W enclosed diode laser will be added alongside it for wood, leather, acrylic and other non-metal work, cutting basswood up to 20 mm in one pass on a 400 x 415 mm working area, with a Creality roller rotary for round items. Dracon also plans to use it for slate and for coated or painted glass.

For heavier plate, a Tracker CNC 4 ft x 8 ft Nitro plasma table will take full 4 x 8 sheets on its 48" x 96" cutting area. Paired with a Hypertherm Powermax85 (PMX85) plasma cutter, it will cut mild steel plate up to 1" thick and pierce up to 3/4". The Powermax85 runs on single-phase power from 200 to 480 V, a good match for the shop's 400 A single-phase service. On 240 V single-phase it will draw about 58 A at full output, and it is rated for a 50% duty cycle at 85 A, or 100% at 66 A. It has digital automatic torch height control, touch-and-retract pierce height control, cutting speeds up to 380 inches per minute, and a downdraft system for smoke extraction. Parts will be designed in Corel Graphics Suite and cut with Tracker's Command and Cut CNC control software.
