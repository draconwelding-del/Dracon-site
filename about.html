About Dracon

A Burin Peninsula company, built on more than 20 years in the trade. Services begin Q3 2027.

Donald Drake, owner and operator

Donald Drake is a Red Seal welder who has worked in the trade since 2003. His career spans major energy and industrial projects, including Coastal GasLink and Suncor's Firebag and Fort Hills sites.

He spent two years at Kiewit Offshore Services in Marystown on the Hebron project's drilling support module. He started on structural welding across many different structures, then moved to the pipe shop, where he welded piping systems in a wide range of grades, thicknesses and materials, including duplex, super duplex and titanium.

At Dracon, pipe and pressure work will be done only under a client's own procedures.

The company

Dracon Welding Services Ltd is a federally incorporated company (CBCA) based on the Burin Peninsula, Newfoundland and Labrador. Our mission is simple: keep industrial welding, fabrication and repair work on the peninsula, done safely and on time.

Our shop at 5A Ropewalk Lane in Burin will be complete in Q1 2027, with move-in in Q2 2027 and services beginning in Q3 2027. Design work will be done through K2 Designs Ltd, a Dracon Group company (incorporation in progress).
