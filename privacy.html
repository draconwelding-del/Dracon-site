Privacy

Short and plain.

No tracking cookies

This site uses no tracking cookies. It counts visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics, a cookie-free counter that does not identify you or follow you to other sites. It records only basic totals, such as which pages are viewed, which site sent the visit, and the general type of browser, device and country, so Dracon can see how people find the site. Some pages show embedded YouTube videos in privacy-enhanced mode; YouTube may set cookies only if you play one.

Interest-form responses

The interest form does not store anything on this site. It opens your own email app, and your answers arrive by email to Dracon. Responses are used only by Dracon and are never shared or sold.

Being removed

To have your information removed, email dracon@draconk2.com and ask.
