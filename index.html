Welding, fabrication and CNC laser cutting, coming to the Burin Peninsula

From Q3 2027, Dracon Welding Services Ltd will offer welding, plate work, repair and parts fabrication close to home. Led by Red Seal welder Donald Drake, with more than 20 years in the trade.

Get in touchPlanned services

Local work, done right

Too much welding and fabrication work still leaves the peninsula. Dracon is building a 40 x 40 ft welding and fabrication shop at 5A Ropewalk Lane in Burin so that work will be done locally, safely and on time.

The shop building will be complete in Q1 2027, we will move in during Q2 2027, and Dracon services will begin in Q3 2027. Nothing is offered before then; this site is for information purposes.

What we will do

Custom fabrication

Frames, skids, brackets, guards and one-off parts, to be built to a drawing or to match a worn original.

Repairs and maintenance welding

Repair of buckets, frames, dump bodies and attachments.

CNC laser cutting

Made-to-print parts, plate kits, laser marking and custom signs on an xTool MetalFab 1200W fiber laser (up to 10 mm carbon steel, 5 mm stainless), plus a Tracker CNC 4 ft x 8 ft Nitro plasma table for plate up to 1" thick (3/4" pierce).

Design through K2 Designs Ltd

From a sketch to a buildable drawing. Design work will be done through K2 Designs Ltd, a Dracon Group company (incorporation in progress).
